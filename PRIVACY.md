## 3. Data Processing

### 3.1 What Data We Process

The Extension processes the following data:

- **Translation preferences**: Your selected source and target languages are stored locally in your browser
- **Text content**: When you press Alt+T, the current text in your input field is processed for translation

### 3.2 How We Process Your Data

**All data processing happens locally in your browser.** The Extension uses your browser's built-in translation API. No text, translations, or personal data is ever transmitted to external servers or third parties.

### 3.3 Legal Basis for Processing

Under GDPR Article 6(1)(f), we process data based on legitimate interest: providing translation functionality that you explicitly request by installing and using the Extension.

## 4. Data Storage

- **Local storage only**: Your language preferences are stored in your browser's local storage
- **No history**: The Extension does not keep any history of your translations
- **No external transmission**: No data leaves your device

## 5. Data Retention

- Language preferences are stored indefinitely until you uninstall the Extension or clear your browser data
- Translated text is not retained after translation is complete

## 6. Third-Party Services

The Extension does **not** use any third-party services, analytics, tracking, or external APIs. All functionality is self-contained within your browser.

## 7. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a data subject under GDPR, you have the following rights:

- **Right of access**: You can view your stored preferences in your browser's extension storage
- **Right to erasure**: Uninstalling the Extension or clearing browser data removes all stored information
- **Right to data portability**: Your preferences are stored in standard browser storage format
- **Right to object**: You can stop processing at any time by disabling or uninstalling the Extension
- **Right to lodge a complaint**: You may contact your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights, you can manage your data through your browser's extension settings, or contact us at the email address above.

## 8. Browser Permissions

The Extension requires the following permissions:

- **Storage**: To save your language preferences locally in your browser
- **Access to all websites** (`<all_urls>`): To enable translation functionality in any input field on any website you visit

These permissions are used exclusively for the Extension's core functionality and no data is transmitted externally.

## 9. Data Security

Since all data processing occurs locally in your browser and no data is transmitted externally, your data is protected by your browser's security mechanisms. We do not have access to your data.

## 10. International Data Transfers

No data transfers occur as all processing is local to your device.

## 11. Children's Privacy

This Extension does not have any age restrictions. No data is collected from users of any age, and all processing is local to the user's device.

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this document with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
